Patents Are for the Weak
========================

by Elon Musk
 https://x.com/elonmusk Elon Musk
September 21, 2022
Original: https://www.cnbc.com/2022/09/21/why-elon-musk-says-patents-are-for-the-weak.html

_During a tour of SpaceX’s Starbase facility with Jay Leno, Elon Musk explained why the company avoids patents._

When Leno asked whether SpaceX had patented the material used to build its ships, Musk replied:

> No, we don’t really patent things. Patents are for the weak.

Musk argued that patents obstruct rather than encourage innovation:

> You know, the problem is patents are generally used as a blocking technique. They’re like using landmines in warfare. They don’t actually help advance things. They just stop others from following you. And most patents are BS.
